Privacy Policy

PSC PYQ is a study companion for Kerala PSC aspirants. This policy describes what we collect and how we use it.

Summary

  • Optional sign-in or guest mode.
  • Signed-in: account, coin wallet, unlocks, referrals, and Pro status on our servers (Supabase).
  • Quiz history and answers stay on your device — not uploaded.
  • Coin packs: Google Play (Android) or Razorpay (web). Pro subscription via Google Play.
  • We do not sell personal information.

Information we collect

  • Account: email; optional name and phone on your profile.
  • Quiz activity: answers, history, badges — stored on your device only.
  • Coins (signed-in): balance, rewards, paper unlocks.
  • Purchases: Google Play or Razorpay verification; Pro subscription status.
  • Local device: theme, preferences, guest coins.
  • Crash reports: Sentry in production (no answers or profile in reports).

Data Compliance

PSC PYQ aligns with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and Google Play data-safety requirements. We act as the Data Fiduciary for personal data processed through the app.

Your rights

  • Access / copy: email geocartindia@gmail.com or view profile in the app.
  • Correction: edit name and phone on Profile.
  • Erasure: Profile → Delete account (see Account Deletion below).
  • Grievance: email geocartindia@gmail.com — we aim to respond within 30 days.

Security & retention

  • HTTPS in transit; Supabase Row-Level Security for account data.
  • Quiz data stays on your device; server account data deleted on account deletion.
  • No sale of personal data. Optional rewarded ads only when you choose to watch.

Account Deletion Policy

Server accounts: Yes — optional sign-in creates an account on our servers.

How to delete (signed-in)

  1. Open the app → Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This removes your profile, coin wallet, unlocks, referral records, and subscription records on our servers, and clears local quiz history on your device. Cancel Google Play subscriptions separately in Google Play.

Guest / local only

  • Uninstall the app, or
  • Android: Settings → Apps → PSC PYQ → Storage → Clear storage.
