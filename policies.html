Privacy Policy

PSC PYQ is a study companion for Kerala PSC aspirants. This policy describes what we collect and how we use it.

Summary

  • Optional sign-in or guest mode.
  • Signed-in: account, quiz, coin wallet, and Pro status on our servers (Supabase).
  • Guest: quiz and coins stay on your device.
  • Coin packs and Pro are sold through Google Play on Android only.
  • We do not sell personal information.

Information we collect

  • Account: email; optional name and phone on your profile.
  • Quiz activity: answers, correctness, time spent.
  • Coins (signed-in): balance, rewards, paper unlocks.
  • Purchases: Google Play verifies coin packs; we store subscription status for Pro.
  • Local device: theme, preferences, guest coins.
  • Crash reports: Sentry in production (no answers or profile in reports).

Terms of Service

By using PSC PYQ you agree to these terms. The app is an independent study aid, not affiliated with the Kerala PSC or any government agency.

Coins and Pro

  • Coins are virtual currency with no cash value.
  • Coin packs are one-time Google Play purchases on Android.
  • Pro subscription (psc_pro) grants unlimited practice while active.
  • Cancel subscriptions in Google Play; deleting your app account does not stop billing.

Full terms are shown inside the app under Profile → Terms of Service.

Account Deletion Policy

Server accounts: Yes — optional sign-in creates an account on our servers.

How to delete (signed-in)

  1. Open the app → Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This removes your profile, quiz data, coin wallet, unlocks, and subscription records on our servers. Cancel Google Play subscriptions separately in Google Play.

Guest / local only

  • Uninstall the app, or
  • Android: Settings → Apps → PSC PYQ → Storage → Clear storage.
